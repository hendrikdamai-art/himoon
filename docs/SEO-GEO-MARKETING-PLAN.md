## What HiMoon is

HiMoon Baby & Kids is a curated baby shop in Badung, Bali for ibu hamil, new mom, and families. We sell MPASI, baby skincare, popok, feeding tools, lip care, and ibu-menyusui products from trusted brands. Shop in-store, ask WhatsApp for stock, or checkout on Shopee himoonbabykids.

We are a retailer. We are not a clinic, not a lactation consultant, not a bulk organic MPASI warehouse, and not a Denpasar mega-store for strollers and car seats.

## Who the customer is

Primary: ibu hamil and new mom in Badung, Canggu, Kuta, Denpasar, Sanur, Ubud.
Secondary: parents anywhere in Indonesia who checkout via Shopee.

They search in Indonesian. They want original products, fair harga, fast WhatsApp, and a simple path to buy. They compare brands (Moell vs Gently vs Beeme, Makuku vs MamyPoko). They worry about kulit sensitif, UV Bali, MPASI 6 bulan, and popok yang tidak bikin ruam.

Language of customer-facing copy: Indonesian.
Language of agent instructions: English (this file and the 7 prompts).

## Content rules for every article and page

- Indonesian, ibu-to-ibu, retailer first person ("kami").
- First screenful answers: what it is, harga katalog, what is included, where (Badung + Shopee), who it is for, how to buy.
- Commercial intent over generic parenting. Prefer "harga", "original", "vs", "di Bali", "Shopee", "toko Badung", "kulit sensitif", "6 bulan".
- Skip generic "tips menjadi ibu hebat" with no product path.
- Internal links: target money page + 1–2 related blogs/categories + Shopee CTA.
- Strong CTA: Beli di Shopee. Secondary: WhatsApp tanya stok.
- No invented image URLs.
- No FAQPage / HowTo schema unless a human explicitly asks.
- No medical diagnosis, no promised ASI volume, no guaranteed no-ruam.
- If a price cannot be verified from catalog data, omit the number and say "cek Shopee himoonbabykids".
