## Tips when sending to your company list

1. Personalise the first line with the company name (from the Excel list).  
2. Prefer a named contact when you have one; otherwise “Hi team at {{Company Name}}” works.  
3. Mention their service type if known (e.g. “as a Melbourne skip bin operator…”).  
4. Add your **booth/stand number** once confirmed by the expo organisers.  
5. Keep BCC empty for personalised outreach; send one-to-one or via your CRM.  
6. Confirm you are registered as an exhibitor and that visitor registration is free before promising that in the email.
